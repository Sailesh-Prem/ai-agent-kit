Sales & Outreach
5 prompts
Prompt 01
Cold DM Writer
Write a 3-line cold DM to [Name], owner of [Business Name], a [type of business] in [city]. I build WhatsApp bots that answer customer questions 24/7 using AI. Sound human, specific to their business, and end with a question — not a pitch. Do not mention price.
Use for: First message to any cold prospect on WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or Instagram.
Prompt 02
Objection Handler
A potential client said: "[paste their exact objection]". I'm selling a WhatsApp AI bot for their [business type] at PKR 12,000 setup + PKR 3,000/month. Write a calm, confident 3-sentence reply that addresses the concern without reducing the price. End with a soft call to action.
Use for: Any time a prospect says "too expensive," "I'll think about it," or "I'm not sure."
Prompt 03
Follow-Up Message
Write a short 2-sentence follow-up message to [Name] at [Business Name]. I sent them a free demo of a WhatsApp bot 48 hours ago and haven't heard back. The message should create mild urgency without being pushy. Mention that I'm taking on 2 more businesses in [city] this month.
Use for: The 48hr follow-up after a demo is sent with no reply.
Prompt 04
Demo Description Writer
Write a 3-sentence WhatsApp message to send alongside a demo link for a WhatsApp FAQ bot I built for [Business Name], a [type] in [city]. The message should explain what the demo does, how to test it, and invite them to say yes to a full build. Keep it conversational and excited, not formal.
Use for: Sending the demo after a prospect says they want to see it.
Prompt 05
Price Anchor Message
Write a confident 4-sentence closing message for a WhatsApp bot service. The setup is PKR 12,000 one-time and PKR 3,000/month maintenance. Frame the price in terms of what it saves them — time and missed customer enquiries. End with "Want to move forward this week?" Do not sound desperate.
Use for: Sending the price after a prospect has seen and liked the demo.

AI Agent Building
4 prompts
Prompt 06
System Prompt Builder
You are a helpful assistant for [Business Name], a [type] based in [city]. Only answer questions using the information below. If a customer asks something not covered, say: "Let me check with the team and get back to you shortly." Always be friendly and reply in the same language the customer uses. Business Info: [paste FAQ, menu, hours, location, pricing here]
Use for: n8n OpenAI node system prompt for any WhatsApp or website chatbot.
Prompt 07
FAQ Extractor
Here is information about [Business Name]: [paste website text, Instagram bio, or any business info]. Extract the 10 most common questions a customer would ask this business on WhatsApp. For each question, write a clear, friendly answer in under 60 words. Format as Q: / A: pairs.
Use for: Building the knowledge base for any client's WhatsApp bot in under 5 minutes.
Prompt 08
n8n Node Debugger
I'm building an n8n workflow and getting this error: [paste error message]. Here's what my workflow is trying to do: [describe the flow]. Here's the node setup causing the issue: [describe the node]. What's wrong and how do I fix it? Give me the exact steps.
Use for: Debugging any n8n error without spending hours Googling.
Prompt 09
Workflow Planner
I want to build an n8n workflow that does the following: [describe the automation in plain English]. I have access to: n8n free cloud, Gmail, Google Sheets, OpenAI API, and Twilio free tier. Design the workflow step by step — list each node, what it does, and what data it passes to the next node.
Use for: Planning any new automation before opening n8n — saves hours of trial and error.

Content Creation
4 prompts
Prompt 10
LinkedIn Hook Generator
Write 5 LinkedIn post opening lines (hooks) about [topic]. Each must be under 12 words. Use one of these formats per hook: 1. Contrarian statement, 2. Specific result with number, 3. Vulnerability/honest confession, 4. List tease, 5. Direct question. Target audience: young Pakistanis learning AI and freelancing.
Use for: Writing the first line of any LinkedIn carousel or text post.
Prompt 11
Content Repurposer
Here is a YouTube transcript: [paste transcript]. Repurpose it into: 1. A LinkedIn carousel hook (2 punchy lines, max 12 words), 2. A LinkedIn caption (150 words, conversational), 3. A 5-tweet thread with a hook tweet, 4. A WhatsApp broadcast message (80 words max), 5. An Instagram caption with 3 relevant hashtags. Keep it punchy — no fluff.
Use for: Turning one video into a week of content for any client.
Prompt 12
Caption Writer
Write a LinkedIn post caption for a carousel about [topic]. The first line must stop the scroll — use a contrarian statement or a specific result. Total length: 100–150 words. End with a keyword CTA: "Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll DM you [freebie name] instantly." Tone: punchy, honest, no corporate fluff. Written by an IT student in Karachi building in public.
Use for: Writing the caption for any LinkedIn carousel post.
Prompt 13
Comment Reply Writer
Write a 2-sentence LinkedIn comment reply to: "[paste their comment]" on my post about [topic]. The reply should add a new insight not already in my post, reference something specific from their comment, and end with a question to continue the conversation. Tone: peer-to-peer, not teacher-to-student.
Use for: Replying to comments on your posts in a way that extends the conversation.

Client Delivery
4 prompts
Prompt 14
Progress Update Writer
Write a short WhatsApp progress update message to a client for whom I'm building a [project type]. Here's what I've completed so far: [list]. Here's what's left: [list]. Expected delivery: [date]. The message should be professional but warm, reassure them everything is on track, and not ask any unnecessary questions.
Use for: Mid-project client updates that build trust without wasting their time.
Prompt 15
Testimonial Request
Write a WhatsApp message asking [Client Name] for a testimonial for the [project] I delivered 7 days ago. The message should: feel natural and not transactional, explain briefly where I'll use it (LinkedIn), make it easy by suggesting they write 2–3 sentences, and thank them for working with me. Keep it under 60 words.
Use for: Requesting testimonials 7 days after every project delivery.
Prompt 16
Payment Reminder
Write a polite payment reminder WhatsApp message to [Client Name]. Invoice amount: PKR [amount]. Due date: [date]. It's now [X days] overdue. The message should be firm but friendly, reference the invoice clearly, and include a simple payment method instruction. Do not apologise for asking. Keep it under 50 words.
Use for: Chasing overdue invoices without damaging the client relationship.
Prompt 17
Delivery Handoff Message
Write a WhatsApp delivery message for a completed [project type] for [Client Name]. Include: a clear statement that it's live, a link to the Loom walkthrough video, instructions for the one thing they need to do to activate it, and an offer to answer questions for the next 7 days. Friendly and confident. Under 100 words.
Use for: The final delivery message that makes clients feel taken care of.

Personal Productivity
3 prompts
Prompt 18
Weekly Review
Here is my activity log for this week: [paste notes or bullet points]. Write a 5-bullet weekly review: 1. What I achieved, 2. What I should have done differently, 3. What wasted the most time, 4. My 3 priorities for next week, 5. One mindset observation. Be direct and honest — not motivational. I don't need encouragement, I need clarity.
Use for: Every Sunday night. Closes the week and opens the next one with focus.
Prompt 19
Daily Priority Decider
Here is my to-do list for today: [paste list]. My current goal is [main goal — e.g., land first client, finish carousel, build workflow]. I have exactly 60 minutes. Pick the ONE task from this list that will move the needle most toward my goal. Explain in 2 sentences why that task and not the others. Ignore everything else.
Use for: Every morning before starting your 60-minute block. Removes decision fatigue instantly.
Prompt 20
Idea to Action Converter
I have this idea: [describe idea]. Turn it into a 3-step action plan I can start today with the tools I already have (n8n free, Gmail, Google Sheets, Notion, ChatGPT free). Step 1 must be completable in under 30 minutes. No theory — just the exact first three actions in sequence.
Use for: Any time you have an idea but don't know where to start. Turns thoughts into tasks in 30 seconds.
