Checklist

Everything you need to land your first paid AI client — in the exact order you need to do it.

your readiness

Build Your Foundation
Do this before sending a single DM
Pick ONE service to sell — not three, not five
WhatsApp bot, content agent, or lead gen automator. One only. You can expand after your first 3 clients.
decision
Pick ONE type of business to target first
Restaurant, clinic, salon, or coaching. One niche = one type of problem = one demo that works for everyone.
decision
Set up your n8n free cloud account
Go to n8n.io → Start for free. No credit card. This is your workshop. Takes 2 minutes.
setup
Build one working demo — even a basic version
Build the simplest version of your chosen service. It doesn't need to be perfect. It needs to work and be demonstrable.
build
Write your one-line pitch
"I build [service] for [niche] in [city] so they can [outcome]. Takes [X] days." Write this down. Say it out loud. Know it cold.
messaging
2
Find Your First 5 Prospects
Takes 30 minutes max
Search your niche on Instagram or Google Maps
Type "salon Karachi" or "restaurant Lahore" — you'll find 50 prospects in 5 minutes. Screenshot their contact info.
prospecting
Pick 5 specific businesses — not a category
Name them. Find their WhatsApp or Instagram DM. Personalisation requires specifics. You need actual names, not a list.
prospecting
Research each business before messaging
Check their Instagram. Notice what they post about. Look for any complaints in reviews. This takes 3 minutes per business and makes your DM 10x more personal.
research
Save your DM scripts to your phone notes
Copy the 5 scripts from the Cold DM Kit. Have them ready. When you're in the moment you don't want to write from scratch.
prep
3
Send the DMs
The part everyone delays — do it now
Send Script 01 — Permission Opener — to all 5
Don't edit it too much. Send it today. The longer you wait, the harder it gets. Done is better than perfect here.
action
Reply within 1 hour to anyone who responds
Speed signals professionalism. Fast replies create momentum. Have Script 02 ready to paste the moment they say yes.
action
Send 48hr follow-up to anyone who didn't reply
Use Script 05. One follow-up only. Not pushy — just the scarcity nudge. This alone recovers 30% of dead leads.
action
4
Close and Deliver
Turn interest into invoices
Build a personalised demo for every interested prospect
Use their business name in the bot. Their menu, their FAQ, their hours. Personalisation closes. Generic demos don't.
build
Quote confidently using the price list below
Don't hedge. Don't say "it depends." Give a clear number. If they push back, hold it for the first 3 clients then raise.
close
Deliver in 3–4 days. Over-communicate every step.
Send a progress update even if nothing changed. Clients who feel informed trust you more and refer you more.
deliver
Ask for a testimonial 7 days after delivery
"Would you mind writing 2–3 sentences about your experience with the bot? I want to share it on LinkedIn." Simple ask. High return. Your next client's first trust signal.
follow-up
Post your result publicly on LinkedIn
"I just delivered my first paid AI project for a [niche] in [city]. Here's what I built and what I learned." That post gets you the next client without any DMs.
