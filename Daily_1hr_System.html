0:00 – 0:10
Block 1 — Review & Plan
10 minutes · do this first, no exceptions
Before you build anything, you need to know what happened yesterday and what matters today. This block prevents you from starting the wrong thing.
Check yesterday's LinkedIn post for comments
Reply to every comment personally. One sentence minimum. This keeps the algorithm pushing your post and builds real connections.
Reply to all LinkedIn DMs from yesterday
Anyone who commented a keyword gets the freebie link + one personalised follow-up question. Don't leave DMs pending more than 24hrs.
Write down today's ONE priority task
Not a list. One thing. Either: write carousel, build workflow, send DMs, or follow up with a prospect. One only.
Check your prospect tracker in Google Sheets
Anyone who needs a follow-up today? Anyone who asked a question and didn't get an answer? Flag them for Block 4.
google sheets
0:10 – 0:30
Block 2 — Build or Write
20 minutes · deep work · phone face down
This is the only block that creates real output. Everything else is distribution and conversation. This block is the work itself — protect it.
Set a 20-minute timer before starting
Use your phone clock. When it rings, you stop — even if you're mid-sentence. This prevents overrun into other blocks.
Work on ONE thing only — no tab switching
If it's carousel day: write all 8 slide headlines first, then fill in content. If it's build day: open n8n and only n8n.
If it's post day — schedule it for peak time
Best times in Pakistan: 8–9am, 12–1pm, 6–8pm. Use Buffer free to schedule. Don't post manually if you can avoid it.
buffer free
Log what you built/wrote in Notion
One line: "Built: WhatsApp bot demo for prospect X" or "Wrote: Day 9 carousel on productivity." This becomes your builder diary.
notion free
0:30 – 0:50
Block 3 — Comment on 5 Posts
20 minutes · your distribution engine · never skip
Commenting is not optional engagement. It's how you get seen. Every comment puts your name in front of someone else's audience. 5 comments per day = 150 new eyes on your name per month.
Search #AIAutomation or #LinkedInPakistan
Find 5 posts with real engagement — not just big accounts. Commenting on niche posts gets you seen by the right people.
Leave a genuine 2–3 sentence comment on each
Add a specific insight, a local angle (Pakistan), and end with a question. Never "great post!" — that helps nobody including you.
Follow the post author if they're in your niche
Builders who post about AI, automation, or sales in Pakistan are potential collaborators, referral sources, and early supporters.
Reply to anyone who replies to your comment
A back-and-forth in someone else's comments is a conversation started. Conversations become connections. Connections become clients.
0:50 – 1:00
Block 4 — Send 3 DMs
10 minutes · one outreach per day compounds to 90/month
3 DMs per day. Not 30. Not 0. 3. At 3 per day, 5 days a week, you're having 60 new conversations per month. That's more than enough to land 2–3 clients every month.
Send 1 new cold outreach DM
Use Script 01 from the Cold DM Kit. One new business per day from your prospect list. Always personalise with their name and business.
Send 1 follow-up to a prospect who went quiet
Check your Sheets tracker. Anyone at "No Reply" for 48+ hours gets Script 05 today. One follow-up only. Never more than twice.
Send 1 value DM to a warm connection
Someone who liked your post, commented, or connected recently. Share a useful resource — your latest freebie, a tool tip, or a workflow idea. No pitch.
Log all 3 DMs in your prospect tracker
Add today's date, message sent, and current status. You can't follow a system you're not tracking. 2 minutes. Do it.
