To Start
your progress
0 / 28
Wk 1
Learn n8n Fundamentals
Build 3 simple workflows · break things · understand nodes
→ 3 working flows
Day 1
Sign up for n8n free cloud
Go to n8n.io → Start free. No credit card. Explore the interface for 30 minutes. Click every menu.
n8n free
Day 2
Build Workflow 1 — Webhook to Gmail
Trigger: manual webhook. Action: send yourself an email via Gmail. 3 nodes. Get comfortable with connecting nodes.
n8n + gmail
Day 3
Build Workflow 2 — Webhook to ChatGPT to Google Sheets
Input text → ChatGPT rewrites it → logs the result to Sheets. This is the core pattern behind almost every AI agent.
n8n + openai + sheets
Day 4
Build Workflow 3 — Scheduled email summary
Trigger: every day at 8am. Action: pull today's tasks from Sheets. ChatGPT formats them. Gmail sends the summary.
n8n schedule + sheets + gmail
Day 5
Learn error handling — add an IF node
Add a condition to one of your workflows. "If the email subject contains X, do Y. Otherwise do Z." This unlocks 80% of agent logic.
n8n if node
Day 6-7
Break, fix, and document your 3 workflows
Intentionally break something. Fix it. Write one paragraph explaining what each workflow does. This becomes your portfolio.
Wk 2
Find the Problem, Validate It
Talk to 5 real businesses · find a confirmed pain point · design your solution
→ validated idea
Day 8
Pick your target niche
Restaurant, salon, clinic, coaching, or e-commerce. One only. Google "most WhatsApp messages businesses get" in that niche.
Day 9
Find 10 real businesses on Instagram or Google Maps
Search "[niche] [your city]". Screenshot names, Instagram handles, WhatsApp numbers. Real businesses. Not directories.
Day 10
Send 5 permission opener DMs
Use Script 01 from the Cold DM Kit. Don't pitch. Just ask if you can share a quick idea. Send today, not tomorrow.
Day 11
Ask the one validation question
To anyone who replies: "What's the most common question you answer on WhatsApp every week?" Their answer is your product spec.
Day 12
Map your solution to their problem
Write it down: "They have this problem → I'll build this workflow → it solves it by doing this." One paragraph. Clear and simple.
Day 13-14
Send 48hr follow-ups + reach 5 more businesses
Follow up anyone who didn't reply. Send 5 more DMs. Numbers are a game at this stage. More conversations = faster validation.
Wk 3
Build the Agent and Demo It
Build your first sellable agent · make it demo-ready · show 5 prospects
→ first demo calls
Day 15
Build a generic version of your agent
Build the WhatsApp FAQ bot (or your chosen product) using a fake business FAQ. Get it working end-to-end first.
n8n + twilio + openai
Day 16
Test it 20 times with real questions
Text your own Twilio sandbox number 20 different questions. Fix every weird response. The demo needs to look flawless.
Day 17
Personalise the demo for your top 3 prospects
Update the system prompt with their actual business name, FAQ, menu, and hours. Takes 30 mins per demo. Closes 3x better.
Day 18
Record a 60-second screen demo video
Use Loom (free). Show the WhatsApp conversation in real time. Narrate what's happening. This is your pitch asset — reuse it forever.
loom free
Day 19
Send the free demo offer to all interested prospects
Use Script 03. Send the personalised demo link or video. No price yet. Just "here's what it looks like for your business specifically."
Day 20-21
Follow up on every demo. Log all responses in Sheets.
Use Script 05 for anyone who went quiet. Track status in a simple Google Sheet: Sent → Replied → Demo Sent → Interested → Closed.
Wk 4
Close, Deliver, and Collect Proof
First paid client · first delivery · first testimonial · first LinkedIn post about it
→ PKR earned
Day 22
Send the price close to your hottest prospect
Use Script 04. State the price clearly. PKR 12,000 setup + PKR 3,000/month. Don't hedge. Ask "want to move forward this week?"
Day 23
Collect payment before starting delivery
Use JazzCash, Easypaisa, or bank transfer. Half upfront minimum. This protects your time and signals commitment from the client.
jazzcash / easypaisa
Day 24-25
Build the live version with their real data
Use their actual WhatsApp number (Twilio live number), their real FAQ, menu, and hours. Test it with 20 real questions again.
Day 26
Send a progress update to the client mid-delivery
"Building is going well — here's a quick look at the bot in action." Even a WhatsApp voice note works. Over-communication = trust.
Day 27
Deliver and hand off with a simple guide
Record a 3-minute Loom showing how to use and update it. Send via WhatsApp. Collect the second half of your payment.
Day 28
Ask for a testimonial. Post your result on LinkedIn.
"Would you write 2–3 sentences about your experience?" Then post: "I just delivered my first AI project in Pakistan. Here's what I built and what I learned." That post gets you client 2.
🎉
Roadmap complete. You have your first client.

Now raise your price by 30% and start Week 1 of the next cycle — this time for client 2.
