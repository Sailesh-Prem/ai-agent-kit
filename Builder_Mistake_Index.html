Pricing Mistakes
3 mistakes
Mistake 01 · Cost: 1 client + 3 weeks
Underpricing to win the deal
Quoted PKR 5,000 instead of PKR 12,000 because I was scared of rejection. Won the deal — but the client treated the work like it was worth PKR 5,000. Endless revisions. Zero respect for timelines. No retainer. Total loss.
The fix
Quote PKR 12,000 minimum, always. Low price attracts low-value clients. The number is a filter — use it.
Mistake 02 · Cost: 2 weeks unpaid
Negotiating down when they pushed back
Client said "that's too much." I immediately dropped to PKR 8,000 without any counter. They accepted but never respected the project timeline. I trained them that my price was negotiable — which meant my time was negotiable too.
The fix
When pushed back on price, hold it for the first 3 clients. Instead say: "The price is fixed but I can adjust the scope if needed."
Mistake 03 · Cost: PKR 0 earned
Doing free work hoping for paid referrals
Helped 3 businesses for free thinking it would lead to paid work or referrals. Got neither. Free work attracts people who don't value the work. One month of effort, zero revenue, zero referrals, and zero testimonials worth using.
The fix
Offer a free demo — not free work. A 2-hour demo closes clients. A month of free work closes nobody.

Client Management Mistakes
3 mistakes
Mistake 04 · Cost: 2 weeks of work
Starting work before collecting any payment
Client agreed verbally. I started building immediately. Spent 2 weeks on a full automation system. They disappeared. No payment. No reply. I had no contract, no deposit, and no recourse. Everything I built was gone.
The fix
50% upfront via JazzCash or Easypaisa before opening n8n. No payment = no project. No exceptions.
Mistake 05 · Cost: 1 referral lost
Forgetting to ask for a testimonial
Delivered great work. Client was happy. I moved on immediately to the next project. Never asked for a testimonial. 2 months later they referred someone to a different person who had more visible social proof. My best work is invisible.
The fix
Set a reminder for day 7 post-delivery on every project. Send the testimonial request that day, every time.
Mistake 06 · Cost: 1 repeat client
Going silent after delivery
Delivered the project. Client said thanks. I went quiet. 3 months later their bot broke from a Twilio update. They called a different developer — not me — because I wasn't top of mind. Lost a PKR 3,000/month retainer forever.
The fix
Check in with every client once a month — one WhatsApp message asking how the bot is performing. This alone retains clients indefinitely.

Content & Brand Mistakes
3 mistakes
Mistake 07 · Cost: 4 weeks of reach
Posting without a defined niche
Posted "AI is the future" content for a month. Motivation quotes. Generic tips. Zero comments. Zero DMs. The algorithm had no idea what I was about — so it showed my content to nobody. Niche-less content is invisible content.
The fix
Post only about AI agents and sales. Every single post. The algorithm rewards topical consistency more than frequency or quality alone.
Mistake 08 · Cost: Zero engagement
Posting the link in the caption
Put the freebie link directly in my LinkedIn caption. Post got 60% less reach than normal. LinkedIn's algorithm buries posts with external links. The people who would have engaged never even saw it. A week of content work wasted.
The fix
Never put links in LinkedIn captions. Use comment CTAs (Comment KEYWORD) and DM the link manually or via freebie tools.
Mistake 09 · Cost: Dead posts
Posting and disappearing for the first hour
Posted carousels and immediately went offline. The first 60 minutes are when LinkedIn decides whether to push your content wider. With zero engagement in that window, every post I made died in the feed before reaching anyone new.
The fix
Stay active for 60 minutes after every post. Reply to comments immediately. Comment on 5 other posts. Engagement in the Golden Hour compounds your reach.

Building & Delivery Mistakes
3 mistakes
Mistake 10 · Cost: 3 weeks
Learning 5 tools instead of mastering 1
Spent 3 weeks trying to learn n8n, Make.com, Zapier, Python, and ChatGPT API simultaneously. Understood a bit of everything. Built nothing. No client would pay for "a bit of everything." Total burnout by week 3.
The fix
Pick n8n. Delete the rest. Spend 2 full weeks on nothing but n8n. Mastery of one tool beats awareness of five, every single time.
Mistake 11 · Cost: 1 angry client
Delivering without testing properly
Delivered a WhatsApp bot to a client without stress testing it. First day live, a customer asked a question slightly differently than the FAQ and the bot gave a completely wrong answer. Client was embarrassed in front of their customer. I lost their trust.
The fix
Before delivery, send 20+ test messages including weird phrasing, typos, and off-topic questions. The bot must handle every edge case gracefully.
Mistake 12 · Cost: Delayed payment
Over-delivering on scope without saying anything
Added extra features the client didn't ask for because I wanted to impress them. They loved it but then expected those extras in every future project at the same price. I created a new standard I couldn't maintain without burning out.
The fix
Deliver exactly what was agreed. If you add anything extra, label it clearly as a bonus. "I added X as a bonus this time — normally this would be an additional PKR Y."

Mindset Mistakes
3 mistakes
Mistake 13 · Cost: 2 months
Waiting to feel "ready" before sending DMs
Spent 2 months learning, building demos, perfecting my pitch — and sending zero DMs. Was waiting to feel confident enough. Confidence doesn't come before action. It comes after the first client responds. I delayed it by 2 months for no reason.
The fix
Send the first DM when you have a working demo — not when you feel ready. Ready never comes. The DM does.
Mistake 14 · Cost: 1 month of momentum
Quitting after 2 weeks of no results
Posted for 2 weeks. Zero comments. Zero DMs. Quit for a month. Started again. Had to rebuild momentum from scratch. The algorithm penalises inconsistency and so does your audience. Stopping and starting is worse than slow and steady.
The fix
Commit to 90 days minimum before judging the results. Results on LinkedIn are exponential — weeks 1–4 feel like nothing. Weeks 5–12 compound fast.
Mistake 15 · Cost: Wasted energy
Comparing my chapter 1 to someone's chapter 10
Spent weeks demoralised because other people on LinkedIn seemed further ahead. They had 5,000 followers and were charging $500/hour. I had 94 connections and was still figuring out my first DM. The comparison paralysed me instead of informing me.
The fix
Only compare yourself to who you were last week. Use other people's journeys as data, not benchmarks. Your path is yours.
